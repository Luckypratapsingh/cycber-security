What is Cyber Security?

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, mobile phones, networks, applications and personal data from unauthorized access and cyber attacks. Strong passwords, software updates, safe browsing habits and awareness can help reduce online risks.

Common Cyber Threats

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or steal information.

🔐

Password Attacks

Attackers may try to guess or obtain weak passwords. Use unique and strong passwords.

💳

Online Scams

Fraudulent offers, messages and websites that attempt to steal money or information.

Cyber Safety Tips

01. Strong Password: Use long, unique passwords for important accounts.
02. Two-Factor Authentication: Enable 2FA whenever it is available.
03. Avoid Suspicious Links: Do not click unknown links or download unexpected files.
04. Update Software: Keep your operating system and applications updated.
05. Protect Personal Information: Do not share passwords, OTPs or sensitive information.
